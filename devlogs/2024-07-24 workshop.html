2024-07-24 workshop

We put on a two day workshop at [boshi's place](boshis.place) in Brooklyn. Basically a demo of how to make plunders using [UnityHawk](/tools/unityhawk.html), then the second part was a jam where we picked three random games and everyone made a plunder using the same three games. Check out all the games made for the jam [here](https://itch.io/jam/plunderludics-workshop-jam/entries).

The first part of the stream was recorded and can be watched here.
